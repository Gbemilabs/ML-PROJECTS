## Research Summary

The existing phase-two work already screened diverse models and retained OOF predictions. Its best result was a nested blend of the baseline, regularized XGBoost, and GradientBoosting: mean fold RMSE 0.123571, standard deviation 0.016617, worst fold 0.150998. The gain is small and fold risk slightly worsened, so it remains unpromoted without repeated baseline comparison or external scoring.

This session found that the saved adversarial-validation AUC of 0.999992 was a preprocessing fingerprint, not real train/test shift. `GarageCars` was converted separately to strings in each data split: integer labels such as `2` in train and float labels such as `2.0` in test. Canonical integer category strings with an explicit `Missing` value reduced exact-pipeline AUC to 0.515209. The old AUC must not be used for weighting or feature removal.

The fix is in both `train.py` and `experiments/phase2.py`. Paired OOF tests show the issue is consequential: test-style decimal serialization worsened pooled XGBoost RMSE from 0.129112 to 0.131353 for the regularized model and from 0.132245 to 0.135323 for the original baseline XGBoost branch. These are controlled format counterfactuals, not measured leaderboard gains.

## Features and Errors

The baseline includes semantic absence categories and house-age, area, bathroom, garage, porch, and amenity features. Phase two added ratios and quality/size interactions. Its repeated-tree and linear/kernel alternatives were weaker individually.

Phase six's current-champion OOF map covers 213 feature and interaction slices (pooled log RMSE 0.123932). ID has no trend (Spearman correlation with champion residual about -0.017). Some low-quality/old slices show overprediction—for example, quality ≤4 and age ≥60 has log bias around -0.072 with 66 training / 76 test homes—but age/quality residual hierarchy failed nested confirmation. Keep these as diagnostic leads, not correction rules.

Baseline OOF residuals show concentration among a few extreme cases. IDs 524 and 1299 are 4,676- and 5,642-square-foot Edwards homes with quality 10, sold for $184,750 and $160,000. Their baseline OOF predictions were about $621k and $505k. Removing them from every regularized-XGBoost training fold improved pooled OOF RMSE from 0.129112 to 0.127188, mainly on other homes. Reproduction artifacts are `experiments/outlier_exclusion_oof.csv` and `experiments/outlier_exclusion_validation.json`, generated by `experiments/phase3_outlier.py`.

Do not globally delete them: test ID 2550 is a close new-construction analogue (Edwards, quality 10, 5,095 square feet, New/Partial sale). Removing the two training rows raised its XGBoost estimate from about $316k to $642k. The evidence supports a rare regime investigation, not a blanket outlier rule.

A fold-local target-mean correction for the exact Edwards/quality-10/recent/New/Partial archetype was evaluated across five seeds of repeated 5-fold partitions using preserved baseline OOF predictions. Eighteen of twenty held-out group appearances had a training peer. Repeated pooled RMSE was 0.125466 without correction and 0.118528 with the peer-only group mean; group RMSE changed from 1.181 to 0.397. The result concerns only two unique training rows and the group was identified after residual/test inspection, so selection optimism is material. The corrected candidate assigns test ID 2550 $171,930 and is not promoted.

A broader, fold-local neighborhood × quality × sale-type/condition × recent-build target encoding was a negative control. All tested smoothing strengths (0.5 to 50) worsened repeated pooled RMSE; the least harmful result was 0.129313 versus 0.125466. A general target-mean blend is rejected.

## Validation and Ensemble

The preserved baseline validation is shuffled 5-fold KFold with seed 42. Its fold standard deviation is large, and pooled OOF RMSE is 0.125466. Existing reports also include target-quantile stratification, repeated folds for selected alternatives, and leave-neighborhood-out diagnostics. A like-for-like repeated evaluation of the full baseline ensemble remains outstanding.

The confirmed external model remains 70% CatBoost + 30% XGBoost. The phase-two three-model nested blend is held. No raw-price, rank, or calibrated blending experiment was externally validated.
